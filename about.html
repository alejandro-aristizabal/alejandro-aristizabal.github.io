About me

I'm an ML Technical Lead on Mercado Libre's AI & Data Platform, based in Colombia, with 7+ years shipping ML products end to end across e-commerce, travel, consumer goods and energy. I care about the unglamorous part: reliable pipelines, reproducible training, observability and the delivery practices that let a team ship models safely and often.

At Mercado Libre I launched a data platform that handles millions of events per minute and now shape the data model behind search and its LLM-based quality evaluation. Before that I led a personalization platform from a one-ship pilot to fleet-wide adoption, co-built a global ML platform used in 8 countries, and more recently I ship retrieval-augmented services and lightweight agents, measured with offline evaluations and production feedback.

I enjoy mentoring engineers and setting technical standards. Outside work I build small products for myself, like an app to practice Latin dances with a webcam and a dish search engine for restaurants in Medellín.
